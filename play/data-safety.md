# Play Data safety answers

Use these answers for the current PulseTime 0.3.0 phone and Wear OS bundles.

- Does the app collect or share any required user data types? **No**
- Is all user data encrypted in transit? **Not applicable; no user data leaves the paired devices**
- Can users request deletion? **Not applicable; PulseTime has no account or server-side data. Uninstalling removes local settings**
- Does the app contain ads? **No**
- Does the app require login or restricted access? **No**
- Does the app use location, contacts, health data, financial data, identifiers, analytics, or advertising data? **No**

The companion sends selected demonstration parameters directly to the paired watch through Google Play services. PulseTime does not collect or retain those commands.

Privacy policy: https://dr.eamer.dev/downloads/apps/pulse-time/privacy/
