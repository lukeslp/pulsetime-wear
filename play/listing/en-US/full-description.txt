PulseTime is an eyes-free clock for Wear OS. Tap the watch app or tile to feel the current time as a short vibration pattern.

Choose from six tactile time systems, from simple hour-and-quarter patterns to exact minutes, binary, and Morse numerals. Adjust playback speed and pulse strength to find a pattern that feels clear on your watch.

The Android companion teaches each system with plain-language explanations, worked examples, watch demonstrations, and replayable tests. Once you know your preferred system, the watch works independently.

PulseTime works offline. It has no account, advertising, analytics, location access, or tracking.

Accessibility features include eyes-free operation, TalkBack labels, large controls in the companion, untimed exercises, and patterns that never rely on vibration strength alone.
