# Play Console app content

- Ads: No, the app does not contain ads.
- App access: All functionality is available without an account, membership, or access code.
- Data safety: No data is collected or shared. See `data-safety.md`.
- Privacy policy: `https://dr.eamer.dev/downloads/apps/pulse-time/privacy/`

- News app: No.
- Government app: No.
- Financial features: None.
- Health features: None. PulseTime communicates time through vibration; it does not diagnose, treat, monitor, or make health claims.
- Content rating: Complete the questionnaire using `content-rating.md` and review Play's generated result.
