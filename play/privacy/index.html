PulseTime Privacy Policy

Last updated November 12, 2026

PulseTime does not collect, store, share, or sell personal information.

The Android companion and Wear OS app work offline. They do not require an account and do not use analytics, advertising, location, contacts, or tracking technologies. Neither app asks for network access.

When the companion sends a demonstration to a paired watch, the command travels directly between the phone and watch through Google Play services. PulseTime does not send that information to any external server.

The watch app stores settings such as the selected time system, playback speed, and vibration strength locally on the watch. The phone companion stores nothing. From version 0.3.2, PulseTime is excluded from Android backup and device transfer, so those settings are not copied to a cloud backup or to a new device. Removing the app removes them.

Questions about this policy can be sent to luke@lukesteuber.com.
