# PulseTime Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 18, 2026

PulseTime does not collect, store, share, or sell personal information.

The Android companion and Wear OS app work offline. They do not require an account and do not use analytics, advertising, location, contacts, or tracking technologies.

When the companion sends a demonstration to a paired watch, the command travels directly between the phone and watch through Google Play services. PulseTime does not send that information to any external server.

PulseTime stores settings such as the selected time system, playback speed, and vibration strength locally on the device. Removing the app removes those settings.

Questions about this policy can be sent through the project's download page.
