First public beta of PulseTime for Pebble, version 1.2.0.

Learn the time by vibration with known-time lessons, replayable practice,
side-button replies, and very large black-and-white digits that follow each
vibration group. Rectangular and round layouts are included.

Please read the first-use steps and known beta limits in the listing. Feedback,
confusing patterns, and ideas for future vibration signals are welcome at
luke@lukesteuber.com. Remote-endpoint connections are not implemented.
